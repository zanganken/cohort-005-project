# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through them lesson by lesson.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back the money they paid for a Purchase. It is **Requested** until someone **Approves** or **Declines** it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback, Pending, Open

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the learner when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation
